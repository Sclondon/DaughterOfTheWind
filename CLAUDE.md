# Daughter Of The Wind: notes for Claude

A Godot 4.7 flying game for the Scareathon arcade, after the flying scenes in Nausicaä.
README.md covers the controls and how it flies.

## Layout

The whole world is built in code. `scenes/main.tscn` is just `scripts/main.gd`.

- `main.gd`: builds the sky, sun, clouds, glider, input, camera and HUD, plus what the level adds: `"clouds"` (cloud sea + fleet) or `"coast"` (`coast.gd`). Whites the view out inside cloud; restart, weather keys, `next_level()` (reloads the scene; the pick is the static `chosen`).
  - Exported switches for tests: `level`, `rider`, `use_fleet`, `use_pads`, `show_hud`. `next_rider()` swaps glider and witch the same way as `next_level()`.
- `glider.gd`: the flight model (Node3D, forward is -Z, integrated in `_physics_process`). Lift and drag from airspeed and angle of attack; the stick asks for a bank angle and an angle of attack. All tuning is the constants at the top.
  - Its air is the sum of `wind_at()` over the nodes in `air`; the nodes in `solids` push it out through `hit()` (signal `bumped`). `control` is the smoothed stick.
  - Upside down with the stick held (`looping`), the roll control keeps the wings level instead of rolling upright, so loops go round.
- `glider_model.gd`: the cream eggshell panel-wing glider (after the user's reference still): a cut-off teardrop tube for a body, wings with a little dihedral and drooped hooked tips, a long pointed pod under each tip joint, two hooked skids, low hoops, electronics housings sunk into the surfaces. Only the flaps move. It poses the pilot each frame (stand / crouch / hang on, from `glider.climb` and the stick; she crouches only in a hard climb, 8 to 17 m/s or the stick past 0.8, and dangles as soon as the glider sinks 3.5 m/s or the stick is pushed: the user found the old thresholds squatted too early and dangled too late). `CREAM` is the shell colour. Each wingtip `trail.gd` ribbon gets its strength from the lift that tip is making.
- `girl.gd` + `models/girl.glb`: the pilot, a single skinned mesh built by `art/build_girl.py` (Blender 4.3: `blender -b --factory-startup --python art/build_girl.py`, then `godot --headless --import`). She is painted in Blender with vertex colours by position (`paint_at`; alpha marks woven cloth for the shader's weave) and wears goggles and a gas mask; lenses are a second material, "glass". Vertex colours go in as written, with no gamma conversion in the script. No animation clips: `pose()` places the hips and solves each arm and leg with two-bone IK, in the glider's space; it takes the centres of the bars her fists close round, not wrist positions (`FIST`, `HAND_TILT`). The model has a pelvis the thighs start inside, fists modelled already gripping (`GRIP`), paint edges cut finer by `refine()`, and goggles, canister and pouch as crisp separate shells (`hard_kit()`); it has no hair or skirt, only a "scalp" paint. She dresses herself in `_dress()`: hair (`locks.gd`), scarf and skirt (`hair.gd`), and `_legs()` gives the skirt its colliders. `art/` has a `.gdignore`; the `.blend` is there to edit by hand.
- `witch_model.gd`: the other rider, a witch on a broomstick (`glider.rider = "witch"`). Same flight, different model; one spark trail from the broom's tail.
- `flight_input.gd`: keys, gamepad and touch into `stick` / `boost` / `brake`. `stick.y` positive pulls the nose up. Tests set `manual = true` and write the values.
- `chase_cam.gd`: follows the glider's interpolated transform in `_process`; keeps the horizon mostly flat when level and goes over with the glider in a loop.
- `coast.gd`: the coast level: sea to the west (-X), endless broken cliffs along Z with cities built into the face (the user wanted "city built into a seaside cliff", not terraced farms), one valley down to a beach with the castle and a grove of great trees. `height_at(x, z)` is the land (`shore_x`, `inland`, `valley`, `trench`, plus buttress ribs and lumps). Narrow sea trenches come every `TRENCH_SPACING` (`trench_index/z/mid/half/floor`; none in stretch 0, the valley's), each with a bridge to fly under. Chunks stream around `focus`; `_work` does one job per call (ground, then props). Also `wind_at()` (ridge lift from `SEA_WIND`). The valley has a stream (`river_z`, repeated in `shaders/terrain`), flower meadows and wobbly fields (terrain shader, driven by vertex colours: red farm, green sand, blue stream).
  - Each chunk keeps its fine height grid (`Chunk.grid`, `_grid_for`, `_on_grid`). The fine mesh, everything in `_build_cliff`, and `surface_at()` / `hit()` all read it, so collision matches the drawn ground (the rough mesh and `height_at()` differ by metres at the ribs).
  - `_build_cliff`: city districts and loose houses (`_add_city_house`, density `city_at`), towers, stairways (`_beam`), viaducts (`_make_arch` spans), rock towers, outcrops (`_make_rock`, bare-rock material). `_add_trench_bridge`: one per trench, with columns and a capsule for collision. Small things go through a bag (`_new_bag` / `_flush`), one MultiMesh per kind. Houses, stairs, viaducts and small outcrops have no collision; spires, towers, windmills, rock towers, big outcrops, great trees and arches do.
  - `shaders/ocean.gdshader` repeats `shore_x()`, the valley, the trenches (`trench_room`) and the sea bed. Change them together.
  - `shaders/backdrop.gdshader`: the painted backdrop inland (giant trees, hills, two mountain ranges): four flats at world x = `BACKDROP_INLAND`, never nearer than `BACKDROP_KEEP`, ray-cast in the fragment shader on a sphere piece that follows the glider (`_build_backdrop`, `_follow`).
  - `shaders/building.gdshader`: house walls with windows sized in metres. `shaders/terrain.gdshader`: the rock is beds, cracks and facets; `all_rock` for outcrops.
- `cloud_manager.gd`: the cloud system. Grid cells per layer (`LAYERS`: cumulus, towers, wisps) stream in around `focus`; each cell is one MultiMesh of puffs, grown from a seed, so a place always has the same clouds. Also the sea of clouds sheet. Towers have `"anvil": true` and grow a thunderhead top, blown along the fixed `ANVIL_LEAN` (not `wind`, so a place regrows the same cloud). Each layer has `"tufts"`: small puffs round a cloud's sides that break off when something passes, drift away and grow back (`INSTANCE_CUSTOM.w` is 1 for a tuft; `Cell.tuft`, `Cell.spans`; tufts are not in `Cloud.puffs`, so they have no density).
  - Cirrus: the `_cirrus` sheet with `shaders/cirrus`, at `CIRRUS_Y` + `altitude_shift`, following the glider; the one blended cloud; cover from `CIRRUS_COVER` per weather.
  - Air queries in world space: `density_at(p)`, `wind_at(p)` (thermals under clouds, gusts inside them, lift over the cloud sea).
  - `set_weather(0..2)`, `prewarm()`, `stats()` (includes `"tufts"`), `all_clouds()`.
  - `stirrers` holds everything that stirs: `stirrer = glider` adds the glider, `add_stirrer(node, radius, length)` adds a hull that shoulders puffs out along its length and leaves a hole for `HOLE_TIME` (`main.gd` adds every ship). A node without `velocity` has it worked out from its movement. Puffs near one are pushed aside and swirled (`_stir`), then settle home. Each cell keeps its puffs' home transforms in `base` and the displaced ones in `stirred` (`[offset, velocity, hold seconds, tuft loose seconds]`; `TUFT_LIFE`, `TUFT_BACK`, `TUFT_GROW`).
  - The puffs' rim is feathered by a slow-noise band (dithered), never a high-frequency discard: the user found the nibbled edge looked "ripped rather than fluffy". Puffs are shaded mostly by their direction from the cloud's middle so a heap lights as one mass, and may carry painted curls (`curls` uniform, 0 turns them off; the user asked for Okami / Wind Waker squirls on clouds).
  - Headless, `MultiMesh.get_instance_transform` returns identity, so tests read the manager's own records.
  - The node itself drifts on the wind; cloud positions are in its space, so subtract `drift` from world points.
- `airship.gd`: one ship built from a length and a seed (`build()`), cruising straight; `hit()` is its collision. The look follows the user's stills in `inspiration art/` (the film's big ships): lavender-grey or rust-red matte paint, and the detail is real fittings, not texture: ribs, pipes, housings, stacks, masts (`_add_fittings()`, one merged mesh), pods on pylons, and glass bubble turrets set into the skin (`_add_bubble()`; `_skin(t, ang)` gives a place on the hull). `shaders/hull` is deliberately sparse (uneven joints with a row of rivets, a line or two per section): the user rejected plate grids ("I dont like the grided look"). Turrets (`_work_guns`) fire at the nearest of `foes` through `flak.gd`, which owns the shells and the Wind Waker-style puff explosions (`shaders/burst`: fire puffs that char, dark smoke that lasts and winds upward, and wreckage on arcs trailing smoke; how much of that a burst gets falls off with its distance from the glider, `CLOSE` / `DETAIL_RANGE`, to stay under `MAX_PUFFS`); only every third turret is a flak gun that turns on the glider, and only inside `GUN_RANGE` (the user asked for the ships to fight each other more than her). A hit on her calls `glider.take_hit()` (5 health, mends; `downed` restarts).
  - Ships have `health` (`max_health` = length x 0.8). Shells carry the side that fired them, and `flak._ship_struck()` turns one that reaches an enemy hull into `ship.take_hit()`. It shows on the floating bar (`shaders/health_bar`) and as smoke from the holes (`flak.smoke()`, puff kind 2). At nothing the ship is `down`: `_sink()` rolls it over and drops it to `floor_y` (the sea or cloud sea, both at 0), and `revive(true)` brings a fresh one down from above into the same place in the line. A falling ship keeps its way on so its slot stays put.
- `diorama.gd` (`scenes/diorama.tscn`): a separate small scene for judging the look: the rider held still in a wind, plain test shapes, a few drifting clouds, a full orbit camera (drag, wheel, pinch) and a movable sun. It is the third entry in `main.LEVELS`; `next_level()` changes scene to it and its Back button returns. Run it directly with `godot --path . res://scenes/diorama.tscn`.
- `battle.gd`: the war. Two `fleet.gd` (one per side, sailing the same circle a broadside apart), one shared `flak.gd`, and `squadron.gd` (the H-shaped fighters, two full wings one ahead of the other joined by the fuselage, since the user said "the H fighters need 2 wings": steered, not flown with the flight model; they fight each other and are shot down and relaunched; only slot 0 of each side will chase the glider, inside `NOTICE`). `battle.armed = false` holds all fire; `battle.place()` moves the whole thing and makes every ship whole again; `battle.ships_lost()` counts sinkings. `main.fleet` is `battle.fleets[0]`.
- `toon.gd` + `shaders/toon.gdshader` + `shaders/toon.gdshaderinc`: the look. Every solid material is cel shaded after Breath of the Wild (custom `light()`: two flat tones, a rim on the lit side, an optional hard highlight; no outlines, **no textures**: the user had a watercolour wash removed on 2026-10-08 as "splotchy"). Use `Toon.paint()` / `tinted()` / `glowing()` / `eggshell()` / `shiny()`, never StandardMaterial3D, for anything lit.
- `hair.gd`: general verlet cloth in world space pinned to an anchor node, with smoothed normals so the two-tone light does not show the triangles (`strands`, `links`, `shape`, `closed`, `stiff`, `solids`, `hem`, `drag`, `weight`, `flutter`). The girl's scarf and skirt (a closed ring kept off her legs by capsules) and the witch's long hair. It is drawn with `Toon.tinted()`; the girl's body paint is used raw by the shader, so pass `color.linear_to_srgb()` to match a body colour.
- `locks.gd`: the girl's shaggy hair: 43 chunky tapered locks in layers from a crown whorl (table in `_ready()`); the top layer is still, the side, nape and stray locks are spring chains in the wind. In group "hair" so the diorama's wind reaches it.
- `sun_glare.gd` + `shaders/sun_glare`: the lens glare, a full-screen additive canvas shader under the HUD. `speed_lines.gd` + `shaders/speed_lines`: lines rushing in from the screen edges at speed or on boost (same layer). Full-screen ColorRects made in code need `set_anchors_and_offsets_preset`, not `set_anchors_preset`, or they stay zero-sized.
- `wind_lines.gd`: Wind Waker-style white wind streaks with a loop, ahead of the camera. `motes.gd`: dust fixed in the world that slides past (one box of motes wrapped round the camera).
- `mesh_kit.gd`: `loft`, `wing`, `slab`, `body`, `rod`. UVs are in metres.
- `shaders/`: `cloud_puff` (MultiMesh puffs; per-puff data in INSTANCE_CUSTOM and COLOR), `cloud_sea`, `cirrus`, `sky`, `hull` (sparse joints and rivets, belly paint, portholes), `terrain` (grass, fields, bedded cliff rock), `ocean` (flat blues, sun glints, trench-aware), `backdrop`, `building`.

## Conventions

- The renderer is Compatibility on desktop too, so what you see matches the web build. No volumetric fog; the clouds are solid puffs with dithered fades.
- The cloud and sea shaders are `unshaded` with `fog_disabled` and do their own haze; the palette is handed down from `main.gd` (`HAZE`, `HAZE_DISTANCE`) and `cloud_manager.gd` (`lit_color`, `shade_color`).
- Nodes moved in `_process` (camera, cloud manager, trails) have physics interpolation turned off. Call `reset_physics_interpolation()` after teleporting anything else.
- Type GDScript vars explicitly wherever inference fails (values from Dictionaries or untyped arrays).
- Comments are plain-English `##` docs at the top of each script plus short inline notes.
- No Python on this machine. For multi-line edits, write a `.cjs` script to `$CLAUDE_JOB_DIR/tmp` and run it with node.
- Only commit or push when the user asks.
- `inspiration art/` holds the user's reference stills (film frames: look at them before restyling the airships). It has a `.gdignore` and is not committed.
